Purpose. The Playground (Patio de Juegos) is a non-profit project that offers free educational games and stories for children, especially for families who cannot pay for apps. There is no advertising, no sales and no subscriptions.

Use. The games are offered as they are, to play at home or in class. We recommend that an adult accompanies the youngest children and keeps an eye on screen time; breaks can be switched on in the parents' area.

Liability. We do our best to make sure the content is correct and suitable for each age. If you find a mistake or anything that seems inappropriate, write to us and we will look at it.

Links. This website does not link to shops or social networks inside the play areas.
