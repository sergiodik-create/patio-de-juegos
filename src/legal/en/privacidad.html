In short: the Playground (Patio de Juegos) does not collect, ask for or send any personal data. There is no sign-up, no advertising, no purchases, no cookies and no tracking tools: we only count visits anonymously.

What is stored and where. To remember the settings (voice, calm mode, text size…), the stars, the stickers and the level of each game, the browser keeps a few lines in the local storage of this same device. That information never leaves the device, we never see it, and it can be deleted from the parents' area (“Delete progress”) or by clearing the site's data in the browser.

Cookies. We do not use cookies. The local storage used is strictly necessary for the settings and progress that the family itself chooses to use.

Voice. Reading aloud is done by the device itself, with its installed voice. No sound is recorded or sent.

Server. Like any website, the server that hosts it ({{alojamiento}}) may log technical data about visits (IP address, date, browser) for its operation and security, under its own policy. We do not use those logs to identify anyone. The fonts and all the files are served from the same site.

Statistics. To know how many visits the website gets, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, from the same company that hosts it. It counts page views and the country they come from, without cookies, without storing anything on the device and without following anyone from one website to another. It cannot be used to identify anyone.

Children. The website is designed for children accompanied by an adult. That is precisely why it collects no personal data at all.
